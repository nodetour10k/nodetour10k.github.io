01 · Framework design

The 300-Point Business Audit

A diagnostic I designed to replace guesswork at the start of every engagement. It scores a business across 29 areas, from branding and website conversion to lead generation, follow-up and sales.

  • 300 checkpoints, each scored missing, weak, working or not applicable
  • Adapts to the business model, so an online store isn't penalized for lacking local listings
  • Maps the score to the right engagement and flags areas to refer to legal, finance or HR partners

02 · Lead generation strategy · demo client

From audit to a revenue plan: mobile auto repair

A worked example, built on a fictional client, showing how the audit becomes a plan. The owner's goals: a full schedule, more repeat customers and business fleet accounts, on a $1,500–2,500 monthly budget.

  • Foundation first: Google Business Profile, listings, review requests and missed-call text-back
  • Four core channels planned with a phase or a reason to wait: CRM, lead generation, SEO, paid ads
  • A seven-stage revenue engine with the weakest link named and a definition of a qualified lead
  • Budget split between fees and media, checked against the client's real budget

03 · Conversion-focused web strategy

Rebuilding the Five Legacy Solutions website

Repositioned a new consultancy's site to read as an established firm and to make the next step obvious for every visitor.

  • One primary action everywhere (request an audit) with call and email always in reach
  • A sticky call-or-request bar on mobile, where most local searches happen
  • Credibility without fabricated proof: a labeled sample scorecard instead of invented testimonials
  • Audit requests delivered to the inbox through authenticated email, not the spam folder
